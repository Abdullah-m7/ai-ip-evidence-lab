# Paper A — Claim–Evidence Matrix

This matrix is the controller gate for the first manuscript. A claim may enter the Results section only if it is supported by a committed artifact or an external authoritative source. Human-effect claims are prohibited until real data exist.

## Status after the Stage 014 controller decision

The Stage 014 controller decision is `NARROW_AND_CONTINUE` (`review/STAGE_014_CONTROLLER_DECISION.md`). Two consequences are binding on this matrix:

1. **Legacy vs corrected profile.** Claims generated under record profile `0.1.0` (legacy profile id `sa-copyright-2026-art26-4-ir30-legacy-v0.1`, `declared_scope_complete=false`) are marked **LEGACY** below. They may appear in the manuscript only as historical proof-of-concept evidence and may never be presented as evidence about the corrected profile `0.2.0` (`sa-copyright-2026-art26-4-art37-1-ir30-v0.2`). Where a corrected-profile claim supersedes a legacy claim, the superseding claim ID is named.
2. **Retired novelty.** The following are **not** claimable as contributions of this paper and must be presented as prior intellectual foundations: a generic missing AI-copyright evidence layer; auditability/reviewability by design; transparency insufficiency; priority for C2PA use in AI-training copyright or rights management; training-data provenance verification generally; and conceptual priority for the term "evidentiary observability". The surviving contribution is the conjunction recorded in claim C30.
